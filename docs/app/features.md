# Features

## Dashboard

OpenVitals starts from a daily dashboard. The dashboard is a scan-friendly summary of the health areas the app supports, with navigation into focused metric screens.

## Period Detail Screens

Metric screens use a consistent period model:

- Day
- Week
- Month
- Year

Each detail screen owns its metric-specific charts, cards, rows, and empty states while sharing the same period navigation shell.

## Activity And Workouts

OpenVitals can show steps, distance, calories, floors, elevation, cardio load, and workout sessions. Activity detail screens can show session metadata, segments, laps, and routes when Health Connect makes them available.

## Sleep And Recovery

Sleep screens show sleep sessions, stage breakdowns, and recovery-oriented summaries such as sleep score and sleep efficiency.

## Heart, Body, And Vitals

OpenVitals supports heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV, blood pressure, oxygen saturation, respiratory rate, body temperature, VO2 max, weight, height, BMI, body fat, lean mass, basal metabolic rate, and bone mass where data is available.

## Manual Entries

Supported manual entries are written directly to Health Connect. OpenVitals keeps local UI preferences, but Health Connect remains the source of truth for health records.
